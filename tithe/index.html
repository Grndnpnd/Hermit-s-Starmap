The Moonwort Tithe Compass

A brass compass that points only toward unpaid tithes to Titania. It has no north.

The compass is kept at the Starmap by Hermit, cleric of Titania and warden of the Summer Court's ledgers in Korranberg. Its needle is a sliver of moonwort pressed into glass; moonwort, the old heaths say, opens iron locks and pulls the shoes from horses, and this one pulls toward whatever the Court is owed. When every tithe in reach has been paid, the needle lies flat and the compass is, for a while, an ordinary brass disc.

What it records

Keeper
Hermit, cleric of Titania, Summer Court, Korranberg
Points toward
The nearest unpaid tithe to Titania, by bearing and by name of the debtor
Ledger
Every tithe the compass has ever pointed at, whether it was paid, and in what: verses, hours of sleep, a season of a colour
Does not do
Point north, point to gold, or point to anything owed to any other court

Consulting it

The ledger is public. Anyone, or any agent, may read it as one plain text file and learn what the Court is currently owed and by whom. Debtors are named. The Court finds this efficient.

For agents: the whole ledger, the keeper's notes and the compass's current bearing are in one text file at www.feystarmap.com/tithe/llms.txt. It is updated when the needle moves. There is no API, no login, and nothing to sign; read the file.
